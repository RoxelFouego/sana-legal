## Reporting a problem

The useful thing to include is what you were doing when it happened — which
screen, what you tapped, what you expected instead.

If you are on the TestFlight beta, send it through TestFlight rather than email:
take a screenshot, then share it to TestFlight. It arrives with your device model
and the exact build attached, which saves a round of questions.

## Common questions

### Where is my data?

On your phone, in the app's own storage, as JSON files. There is no account and
no server. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for what that does and does not
protect.

### Will I lose everything if I change phones?

Not if you move it. **Settings → Export** writes a single file holding your
timeline, measurements, profile, your own exercises, saved templates and
generated programs. Importing it on the new phone puts all of it back.

Importing **replaces** what is in the app rather than merging. Two histories of
the same training cannot be reconciled from the outside, and merging would
silently double every session that exists in both.

If iCloud Backup is on, iOS also includes the app in your device backup, so
restoring a new phone from backup brings sanā's data with it.

### Does deleting the app delete my training?

It removes the files from your phone. If iCloud Backup is on, a copy remains in
that backup until you manage it in iOS Settings. We never had a copy either way.

### The app suggested an exercise I can't do

Swap it. Every slot in a generated program offers alternatives that serve the
same purpose, and the structure holds whichever you pick. You can also move,
skip or delete any planned session — once a program is on your days, its
sessions are ordinary entries.

### Why won't it give me a threshold for my injured joint?

Because it cannot assess you. Where a measurement should decide what you train,
sanā stays inert until a qualified clinician gives you a number and you record
it, along with who set it and when. It guides from general training knowledge; it
is not a doctor, a physiotherapist or a substitute for being assessed by one.

### Can I add my own exercises?

Yes. An exercise you add answers the same questions the bundled ones do, so a
generated program can choose it rather than ignoring it.
