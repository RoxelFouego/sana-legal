# sanā Privacy Policy

**Effective and last updated:** October 25, 2026


## Plain-Language Summary

sanā keeps your training on your phone. Not as a feature we might change later —
as the way it is built.

- There is **no account**, no sign-up, and no password. We never learn who you are.
- There is **no server**. Your training history, your measurements and anything a
  clinician records are files in the app on your device. They are not copied
  anywhere by us.
- There are **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers** in the app.
- We **cannot** read your data, because it never reaches us. This is not a promise
  about our conduct; it is a consequence of there being nowhere for it to go.
- You can **export everything** as JSON whenever you want.

One honest caveat, covered properly in section 4: if you have iCloud Backup
switched on, iOS includes this app's files in your device backup. That backup is
between you and Apple — we have no access to it — but it does mean your data
exists somewhere other than the phone in your hand, and deleting the app does not
remove it from there.

## 2. What We Collect

**Nothing.**

Not an anonymised subset, not aggregate usage statistics, not crash reports. The
app makes no network requests of any kind.

Our App Store privacy disclosure reads **Data Not Collected** across every
category, and that is a complete description rather than a summary.

## 3. What the App Stores on Your Device

All of it stays on your phone. We list it so you know what exists, not because we
can see it:

| What | Examples |
|---|---|
| Your training log | sessions, exercises, loads, reps, times, distances, notes |
| Your measurements | bodyweight, steps, resting heart rate, HRV, breath-hold, joint range |
| Clinical thresholds | a number a physiotherapist gives you, with who set it and when |
| Your profile | the name you type, height, training experience, units, joints you are managing |
| Things you create | exercises you add, templates you save, programs you generate |

These are ordinary files in the app's own storage, in a documented JSON format.

**Health data.** Some of the above is health information, and we treat it that
way. The protection it gets is structural: it is on your device, we have no copy,
and there is no mechanism by which we could obtain one.

## 4. iCloud Backup — Read This One

If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, iOS includes this app's files in your
device backup. We do not control this, we cannot see that backup, and we are not
told it exists.

What it means in practice:

- Your training data is stored by Apple, under your Apple Account, governed by
  [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and not by this one.
- **Deleting the app removes its files from your phone, but not from that backup.**
  To remove them there, manage your backups in iOS Settings.
- Restoring a new phone from backup restores your training history with it, which
  is usually what people want.

We are spelling this out because "uninstall deletes everything" is the kind of
claim privacy policies make without checking. It is not true on iOS when backup
is on.

## 5. What We Never Do

- We never show advertising.
- We never send anything to an AI or analytics service. The app has no network code.
- We never ask for your email, your contacts, your location or your photos.

## 6. Getting Your Data Out

Export produces a single JSON file containing your full history — the timeline,
your measurements, your own exercises and templates, and the clinical thresholds
with their provenance. It is yours to keep, read or move elsewhere.

JSON is currently the only export format.

## 7. Your Rights

Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and equivalents elsewhere give you rights of access,
correction, deletion and portability over personal information a company holds
about you.

**We hold none**, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. In
practice:

- **Access and portability** — use Export. The whole file is yours.
- **Correction** — edit any entry directly in the app.
- **Deletion** — delete entries in the app; deleting the app removes its files
  from the device. If iCloud Backup is on, see section 4.

If you believe we hold information about you, write to **hello@entelana.com** and we
will tell you honestly what we have, which we expect to be nothing.

## 9. If This Ever Changes

We may add features that need a network — syncing across your devices, or a
shared kitchen module. We are not promising never to. We are promising how:

1. **Opt-in, always.** Anything leaving your device requires you to turn it on,
   and the app stays fully usable if you never do.
2. **We will say exactly who can read what, before you turn it on.** Not
   "securely stored" — the actual answer, including whether we or a provider such
   as Apple hold a key that could decrypt it. If we cannot promise that only you
   can read it, we will say so plainly rather than implying otherwise.
3. **No retroactive change.** Data already on your device is not uploaded because
   a policy changed. Only data created after you opt in is affected.
4. **We will tell you in the app**, in plain language, before it takes effect.

We have deliberately not promised zero-knowledge encryption here. It is a specific
technical guarantee, the obvious sync route on iOS does not provide it by default,
and a promise made now that the architecture breaks later is worse than no promise.
