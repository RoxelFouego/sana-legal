## 3. Health and Fitness — Read This Section

**sanā is information, not advice.** It provides exercise descriptions, training
structure and a record of what you did, for general informational purposes. It is
not a medical device. It does not provide medical, clinical, physiotherapeutic or
nutritional advice, and it does not diagnose, treat or assess anything.

**It cannot see you.** The app does not know how you are moving, how you feel, or

**Where a clinical judgement is required, the app refuses to make one.** By
design, sanā never invents a clinical threshold. Where a measurement should gate
what you train, the app stays inert until a qualified clinician gives you a
number and you record it, along with who set it and when. We built it this way on
purpose, and you should treat anything it says in the same spirit.

**Consult a professional.** Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before
